## Search

Pick a source with the button on the left of the search box (TheTVDB, AniDB, TheMovieDB::TV or TVmaze;
`Ctrl`+`↑`/`↓` switches it), type a show and press **Find** or `Enter`. Each search opens in its own tab.

| Option | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Season | **All Seasons** or a single season (`Shift`+`↑`/`↓`) |
| Order | Airdate, Official, DVD, Absolute, AbsoluteAirdate, Digital, StoryArc or Production (with TheTVDB, Official is the Aired Order, Digital the Alternate Order, StoryArc the Alternate DVD Order and Production the Regional Order, and Airdate follows the default order of the series; with TheMovieDB, Digital, StoryArc and Production are episode groups. Series without that order use the default one) |
| Language | Language of the episode titles |

## Use the list

Right click the list:

- **Send to** › **Rename** uses the episodes as new names.
- **Double Episodes** joins consecutive episodes of each season in pairs (`S01E01-E02`, `S01E03-E04`, ...), for files that hold two episodes each. **Single Episodes** splits them again.
- **Copy** puts the selected episodes on the clipboard.
- **Save as ...** writes the list to a text file.

You can also drag the list onto a folder, another app or a panel of the navigation.

The **History** tab keeps your recent searches.
