## Search by name

Type a movie or show, pick a language (or **All Languages**) and press **Find**. The languages you used last are
listed first, under **Recently used**.

OpenSubtitles keeps three kinds of Spanish subtitles: **Spanish** (most of them, from Spain and Latin America alike),
**Spanish (EU)** and **Spanish (LA)**. **Spanish** searches Spanish and Spanish (EU), and **Latin American Spanish**
searches Spanish (LA) and Spanish, with the regional variant listed first. On the command line, use `--lang es` or
`--lang mx`.

1. Double click a subtitle package (or press `Enter`) to download it.
2. Its files appear below. Double click one (or press `Enter`) to preview it.
3. Right click a file to **Preview**, **Save As...** or **Export...** it. **Export...** writes SubRip (`.srt`) files in
   UTF-8 to a folder you choose.

Without an account you can download 5 subtitles per day. **Login** signs in with your
[OpenSubtitles.com](https://www.opensubtitles.com) account to download more: 20 per day with a free account, up to
1000 with VIP. The command line uses the same login (`filebot-pro -script fn:configure`).

## Subtitles for your videos

<Steps>

1. Drop the video files or folders onto the **download** button at the top right of the panel, or click it to
   select a folder.

2. FileBot Pro computes a hash of each video and searches for subtitles made for that exact release. The search
   needs the complete video: a partial download or a re-encoded copy finds nothing, so use the search by name instead.

3. In the dialog, pick the subtitles to save next to the videos.

</Steps>

For Chinese subtitles it also searches Shooter (射手网), which needs no key.

:::note
The OpenSubtitles.com API does not support uploading subtitles. Upload them on
[opensubtitles.com](https://www.opensubtitles.com) instead.
:::
