---
title: Subtitles
description: Find subtitles by name or for your video files, and share your own.
---

:::note
Subtitles come from OpenSubtitles and work without any setup.
:::

## Search by name

Type a movie or show, pick a language (or **All Languages**) and press **Find**.

1. Double click a subtitle package (or press `Enter`) to download it.
2. Its files appear below. Double click one to preview it.
3. Right click a file to **Save As...** or **Export...** it.

Without an account you can download 5 subtitles per day. **Login** signs in with your
[OpenSubtitles.com](https://www.opensubtitles.com) account to download more: 20 per day with a free account, up to
1000 with VIP. The command line uses the same login (`filebot-pro -script fn:configure`).

## Subtitles for your videos

Drop video files or folders onto the **download** button at the top right of the panel, or click it to
select a folder. FileBot Pro computes a hash of each video, finds subtitles made for that exact release and
shows them in a dialog where you pick the ones to save next to the videos. For Chinese subtitles it also
searches Shooter (射手网), which needs no key.

:::note
The OpenSubtitles.com API does not support uploading subtitles. Upload them on
[opensubtitles.com](https://www.opensubtitles.com) instead.
:::
